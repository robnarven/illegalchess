How Illegal Chess works

Normal chess rules, plus the illegal stuff below. You play against the bot, and it knows (and uses) every rule here too. You can open this again any time with the i button.

Horse riding

Pawns and rooks can ride your knights. Drag a pawn or rook onto one of your own knights; it has to get there with its normal move (rook in a straight line, pawn one step forward or diagonally forward).

  • Drop the horse on a knight-jump square and the rider comes along.
  • Drop it on a square the rider could reach and the rider hops off; the knight stays.
  • A pawn hopping off onto the last row promotes. If the horse is captured, the rider dies too.

The queen's crown

Grab the queen by her crown (top of her square) and drop it on one of your pieces' heads. That uses your turn.

  • The crowned piece keeps its own moves and moves like a queen.
  • The queen becomes a queenless queen that moves 1 square, like a king.
  • Capture the queen or any crowned piece and your piece steals the crown.

Quests

Your weapons start locked. Complete the quest shown at the top (like "Bowl a piece" or "Capture the enemy queen") to earn them.

  • 1st quest: the pistol. 2nd: the RPG. 3rd: the banana peel. After that each quest tops up whatever you have least of: +1 bullet, +1 peel or +1 rocket (max 3 each).
  • Lose a weapon and the next quest gives it back.
  • Quest cut off with "..."? ClickTap the quest box to read all of it.

Pistol

Earned from a quest. Starts with 1 bullet (quests add more, up to 3).

  • Drag it onto any of your pieces (free, doesn't use your turn). It stays with that piece.
  • Aim with your mouse and press S to shoot.Tap Shoot, tap a target to aim, then tap it again to shoot. Shooting uses your turn.
  • Shoot any enemy your piece can see, in any direction and at any angle, as long as no piece is in the way. Kings can't be shot.

RPG

Earned from a quest. Starts with 1 rocket (quests add more, up to 3). Drag it onto one of your pieces (free, it stays with that piece).

  • Aim with your mouse and press R to fire the rocket.Tap Fire, tap a target to aim, then tap it again to fire. Firing uses your turn.
  • The rocket flies over pieces, so it can hit any enemy piece. The blast destroys the target and every enemy piece around it (the 3×3 area). Kings are safe.

Banana peel

Earned from a quest (quests add more, up to 3 peels). Drag a peel from your inventory onto an empty square. Placing it is free, on your turn.

  • The next enemy piece that steps there slips and falls down. It stays down for 1 move (the bot can't move it), then gets back up.
  • The bot can't see peels. Your own pieces don't slip, and kings just squash them.

Pawn bowling

Double-clickDouble-tap the little ball on top of one of your pawns. It pops off and rolls to a random enemy piece ahead of the pawn that it has a clear path to, knocking it over.

  • Only one bowl per game; the pawn that bowls is left headless. Bowling uses your turn.
  • The ball can't pass through pieces.
  • The bot gets one bowl per game too, starting at a random point in the game.
  • Kings can't be knocked over.

The Void

Double-clickDouble-tap the slash on one of your bishops, then pick an enemy piece up to 2 squares away.

  • The slash flies to that square and opens a black hole that sucks the piece in, then closes.
  • Once per game (the bot gets one too). Kings can't be sucked in. Uses your turn.
  • After that, your bishop's slash is gone and it just has a dot.

Aaaaaaaah

When a piece gets captured (yours or the bot's), it falls over and lies on the floor. Pieces can walk over it. The bot's king can revive its fallen pieces too.

  • Drag your king onto a fallen piece next to it (the square must be empty). The king's cross stabs it, heavenly light shines down and it comes back to life.
  • The price: your king loses its cross and can't move diagonally anymore. Uses your turn.
  • Your king can only revive once per game. After that, fallen pieces stay dead.

Looks & controls

  • Bishops look like pawns, with a slash on the head.
  • Drag the 🚫 off the logo, then drag the horse onto an empty square on your turn: you get an extra knight. Free, once per game.
  • Pieces move by dragging only (with your finger; the piece lifts above your finger and lands on the square it's over). No move hints, except when you're stuck in check (below).
  • Grabbing the queen by her crown and dropping it on an empty square just moves the queen.
  • Stuck in check for 20 seconds? The game shows you where to move, with glowing rings.
  • Pick the bot's level at the top: Easy, Medium or Hard.
  • After a game, Review game lets you step through every move (⏮ ◀ ▶ ⏭).
  • An update or a quick refresh doesn't reset your match or your tutorial. Coming back later starts a fresh match.
  • The Code box at the top is for secret codes. We're not telling.
  • The 🌐 button changes the language.
  • Very rarely (1% of games), a 🛸 UFO flies in and abducts a random piece. Any piece. If it takes a king… the UFO wins.
  • Weather comes every 1 or 2 rounds (other rounds are ☀️ Clear): 🌬️ Windy (pieces lean a tiny bit left in the wind), 🌫️ Foggy, or 🌧️ Rainy (raindrops fall and some leave puddles; a piece landing in a puddle has a 10% chance to slip and pop right back up).
  • R fires the RPG rocket and S shoots the pistol, at wherever your mouse is pointing.
  • F flips the board (turns it around so you see it from the other side).
  • ← → step through the game's moves, New Game restarts.
  • New Game (top right) restarts the game. Turning your phone sideways gives a landscape layout.
